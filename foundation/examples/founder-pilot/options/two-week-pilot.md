# Option

Offer a limited weekday lunch menu for pre-order and collection over ten operating days.

## Advantages

- Lower fixed commitment than a lease.
- Tests payment behaviour rather than stated preference.
- Produces operational data about demand, waste and contribution margin.

## Trade-offs

- Small pilot sample.
- Existing facilities may not represent the economics of a permanent site.
- The pilot may require temporary permissions or partner arrangements.
